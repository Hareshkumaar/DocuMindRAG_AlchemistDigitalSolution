Northwind Labs – Leave Policy FAQ (fictional sample document)

Q: When does annual leave reset?
A: Annual leave resets on 1 January each year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over until 31 March.

Q: Can I take leave during probation?
A: Yes. Leave accrues from your first day, but leave during probation needs approval from both your manager and HR.

Q: How do interns request leave?
A: Interns request leave through the HR portal in the same way as employees. Interns earn 1 day of paid leave per completed month.

Q: What happens to unused leave when I leave the company?
A: Unused annual leave from the current year is paid out in the final salary. Carried-over days and sick leave are not paid out.

Q: Is there a limit on consecutive leave days?
A: Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need approval from the department head.

Q: Who do I contact about leave questions?
A: Contact the HR team through the HR portal or at the HR help desk on the second floor.
